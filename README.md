# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis is a data analysis project that studies agricultural performance across different seasons, crops, geographical areas and farming conditions.

The project uses a dataset containing 4,000 farm records and analyzes important factors such as crop yield, production, revenue, profit, irrigation, water usage and disease/pest risk.

## Objectives

- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Compare crop yield, production, revenue and profit.
- Study the performance of different irrigation methods.
- Compare agricultural performance across states.
- Examine water usage, water efficiency and disease/pest risk.
- Identify meaningful patterns and seasonal variations.
- Generate data-driven insights and recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 4,000 agricultural farm records with 28 attributes covering:

- Farm and geographical information
- Crop and season
- Environmental conditions
- Soil and farming practices
- Irrigation and resource usage
- Crop yield and production
- Revenue, cost and profit
- Water efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## Data Cleaning

The dataset was checked and prepared before analysis.

- Missing values were identified and handled using median imputation.
- Duplicate records were checked.
- Farm IDs were checked for uniqueness.
- Categorical and numerical data were examined.

## Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Average yield by season
- Average profit by season
- Average yield and profit by crop
- Irrigation method performance
- Water efficiency
- State-wise agricultural performance
- Revenue by crop
- Water usage by season
- Disease/pest risk by season
- Relationships between environmental/farming factors and crop yield

## Key Findings

- Kharif recorded the highest average crop yield at approximately 5.63 tonnes/ha.
- Kharif generated the highest average profit at approximately ₹1.79 lakh.
- Sugarcane had the highest average yield and average profit among the crops in the dataset.
- Drip irrigation recorded the highest average yield and average profit among irrigation methods.
- Rainfed farming recorded the highest average water efficiency.
- Punjab recorded the highest average yield among the states in the dataset.
- Kharif had the highest average disease/pest risk.
- Zaid recorded the highest average water usage.
